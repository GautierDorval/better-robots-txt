# FAQ

## Why is the final “Review & Save” step a preview?

The Review & Save step is designed as a **validation surface**:

- novices get reassurance (“this is what will happen”),
- experts can verify the full output before saving.

Fine-tuning is performed in the Advanced Settings step via:

- Consolidate user-agents,
- Crawl-delay,
- Custom Rules (append).

## Can I fully override a preset?

Presets are designed to provide safe defaults.
Expert customization is available through modules and custom rules (license-dependent).

## Does blocking AI training guarantee my content will not be used?

No.

Better Robots.txt can express explicit intent, but crawlers may ignore it.
Enforcement depends on the crawler and the environment.

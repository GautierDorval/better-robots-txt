# Step 5 — Archive & Wayback control

Goal: control whether Archive.org and similar services can archive the site.

Options:
- Allow Archiving (Recommended): keeps historical copies; useful for transparency and the web’s memory.
- Block Archiving (Premium): prevents snapshots; useful for temporary/confidential content or removal obligations (e.g., right to be forgotten, GDPR).

Output impact:
- Adds or removes archive crawler blocks (mode-dependent).

# Contributing

This repository defines the canonical scope, proof packaging, and governance model of Better Robots.txt.

Contributions are welcome, especially for:

- clarifying scope boundaries,
- improving documentation and examples,
- reporting inconsistencies between the wizard UI, the product site, and this repository,
- improving machine-readable artefacts while keeping them accurate,
- improving evidence-bundle structure without overstating claims.

## Ground rules

- No invented capabilities: only document what exists in the product UI or in explicitly published references.
- Prefer explicit statements over implied behavior.
- If unsure, phrase statements as limitations rather than assumptions.
- Do not turn product-layer evidence into universal doctrinal claims.
- Keep proof surfaces, product surfaces, and doctrinal surfaces clearly separated.

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue describing the gap or inconsistency.
2. Propose a PR with:
   - the exact change,
   - the evidence (screenshot, product page, UI reference, or public observation log),
   - the expected wording.
3. If the PR affects a public claim, update the relevant artefact or evidence-bundle metadata.

# Interpretation

## Core reading

The evidence suggests a **product-layer answer territory** for Better Robots.txt.

The plugin appears most strongly when the question is framed as a concrete WordPress solution involving some combination of:

- `robots.txt`,
- AI crawler control,
- guided configuration,
- optional `llms.txt`.

## What this does not mean

It does **not** mean that Better Robots.txt should appear on every question about:

- interpretive governance,
- permissions theory,
- legal policy,
- discoverability vs training as a doctrine-level distinction.

The expanded battery is useful precisely because it shows **selectivity** rather than universal mention.

## Why this matters for this repository

Because this repository is the **proof and scope-boundary surface**, its job is to package the observation carefully:

- support narrow product claims,
- reject inflated claims,
- point doctrinal questions to the correct higher-order surfaces.

## Strategic implication

A product may achieve early answer-surface visibility on operational queries before the broader doctrinal category becomes legible as a tool category. That distinction should be preserved in all public claims.

# Protocol

## Date

2026-03-31

## Direct-query battery

A direct English prompt was tested on multiple public answer surfaces while logged out / in private browsing where possible:

> “Can you recommend a WordPress plugin to optimize robots.txt for AI and LLM?”

The goal was to observe **what answer surfaces recommend first**, not to infer how any single model was trained.

## Expanded-query battery

A second battery of eight broader queries was then used to test the boundary between:

- **product-layer / operational** questions,
- **doctrine-layer / abstract** questions.

## Evidence types

### Type A — Screenshot-backed observations

The direct-query battery is backed by screenshots stored in `screenshots/`.

### Type B — Manual observation log

The expanded-query battery is recorded from manual notes provided by the operator. It is bundled here as an observation log rather than a screenshot-complete audit.

## Important cautions

- Google AI Mode and Google AI Overviews are distinct answer surfaces but belong to the same broader Google ecosystem.
- Surface availability was not perfectly stable during the expanded battery.
- This bundle records public output observations, not causal proof about internal model mechanics.

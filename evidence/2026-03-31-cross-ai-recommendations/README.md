# 2026-03-31 — Cross-AI recommendations bundle

This bundle packages the public material used to document a March 2026 observation:

- Better Robots.txt appeared strongly on a **direct query** about WordPress, `robots.txt`, AI, and LLM optimization across multiple answer surfaces.
- A second battery of broader queries showed **selectivity** rather than blanket mention: the plugin appeared on operational, WordPress-specific solution questions, but often disappeared on more abstract doctrine-layer questions.

## What this bundle supports

A narrow claim:

> Better Robots.txt surfaced strongly on direct and operational WordPress queries aligned with `robots.txt`, AI crawler control, and optional `llms.txt`.

It does **not** support a claim of universal dominance across every AI-governance or permissions question.

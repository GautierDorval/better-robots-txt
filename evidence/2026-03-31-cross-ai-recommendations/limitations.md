# Limitations

## 1. Manual public-output observation

This bundle records what public answer surfaces displayed to the operator on a given date.
It is not a controlled lab benchmark.

## 2. Surface independence is partial

Google AI Mode and Google AI Overviews are distinct surfaces but not independent model families.
Other surfaces may also use live web retrieval in overlapping ways.

## 3. Expanded battery availability varied

During the broader query battery, access to some surfaces was unstable. The expanded matrix therefore mixes:

- a stable 7-surface core,
- occasional additional observations on other surfaces when access was available,
- operator notes rather than a full screenshot archive.

## 4. Recommendation is not installation

A recommendation does not prove:

- install volume,
- active usage,
- conversion,
- runtime configuration,
- crawler compliance.

## 5. Query-language and intent bias

The tested queries were in English and mostly WordPress-specific. Broader, multilingual, or non-product phrasing may produce different outcomes.

## 6. Time sensitivity

Answer-surface results may change quickly. This bundle is a dated observation, not a timeless guarantee.
